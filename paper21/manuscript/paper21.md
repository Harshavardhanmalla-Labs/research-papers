# AI-Enhanced Endpoint Compliance and Automated Vulnerability Management Framework for Essential Government Infrastructure

**Harshavardhan Malla** — Arizona Department of Transportation, Phoenix, AZ, USA

> **Status: Published** — *Journal of Engineering Research and Sciences (JENRS)*, Vol. 3, Issue 1, 2024.
> **DOI:** [10.55708/js0304001](https://dx.doi.org/10.55708/js0304001). Full paper in the **Submission** tab (PDF).

## Abstract

Public sector IT infrastructures that underpin essential services, such as transportation and law enforcement, are becoming progressively susceptible to advanced cyber attacks and encounter heightened regulatory demands, especially in accordance with CJIS and NIST standards. Existing methods for compliance enforcement and patch management are primarily manual or only slightly automated, thereby constraining their scalability, precision, and adaptability.

This paper presents an AI-augmented cybersecurity system that integrates compliance detection, vulnerability prioritization, automated remediation, and disaster recovery. The system employs a hybrid methodology for compliance detection, combining rule-based logic with XGBoost-driven anomaly categorization, and uses telemetry data to highlight vulnerabilities. It automates patch deployment with SCCM and PowerShell, and integrates predictive disaster recovery orchestration with real-time audit dashboards. In a simulated government network with 10,000 varied endpoints, the framework exhibited 92% accuracy in compliance detection, a 40% reduction in patch deployment time, and a 70% drop in disaster recovery delay.

**Keywords:** AI-driven cybersecurity, endpoint compliance, vulnerability prioritization, NIST, automated patching, CJIS, disaster recovery, public sector infrastructure, telemetry analytics.

## Key results

- **92% accuracy in compliance detection** via a hybrid rule-based + XGBoost anomaly classifier.
- **40% reduction in patch deployment time** through SCCM + PowerShell automation.
- **70% reduction in disaster recovery delay** via predictive DR orchestration.
- Evaluated on a simulated government network of **10,000 heterogeneous endpoints**.
- Real-time **audit dashboards** for continuous compliance monitoring (CJIS / NIST).

## Contribution

A single AI-augmented framework that unifies four operations historically handled in isolation — compliance detection, vulnerability prioritization, automated remediation, and disaster recovery — for mission-critical public-sector infrastructure. The work demonstrates that hybrid (rule + ML) compliance detection with automated patch orchestration markedly improves the scalability, resilience, and auditability of government cybersecurity operations.
