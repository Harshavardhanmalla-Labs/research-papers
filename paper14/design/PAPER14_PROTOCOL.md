## 1. Motivation

On the second Tuesday of each month, vendors disclose a batch of CVEs and a government patch
team must decide, at disclosure time, which to deploy first under a fixed remediation capacity.
The natural assumption is that the exploit-likelihood signal should drive that decision. But at
disclosure time the Exploit Prediction Scoring System (EPSS) score is immature: it rises over
the days after disclosure as exploit evidence accrues, and Known Exploited Vulnerability (KEV)
entry lags disclosure. The decision must be made on a noisy, biased-low early estimate.

This paper asks which signal actually governs good disclosure-time triage for a government
fleet: the early exploit estimate, or the asset context (criticality, exposure, sensitivity) of
the host the CVE sits on. The pre-registered finding we set out to test is that asset
criticality dominates, and that the immature day-0 EPSS observation contributes little beyond
stable signals that are already available at disclosure (intrinsic severity and the partial
KEV-at-disclosure flag). The practical implication, if confirmed, is that teams should weight
the patch queue by mission context immediately rather than waiting for EPSS to mature.

Because a real EPSS time series is not available, the disclosure-time EPSS observation is
generated by a pre-registered maturation model: the real corpus EPSS is the matured value at
horizon H, and the day-0 observation is a noised, biased-low draw from it. The maturation noise
is swept. Limits are stated in Section 8.

## 2. Hypotheses

**H1 (Primary).** Context-weighted disclosure-time prioritization (PTI) exceeds a context-blind
day-0 EPSS baseline on mission-weighted precision for emergent-high-risk CVEs by at least 5
points at triage capacity (K = 50, reference noise sigma = 0.50), across 25 evaluation seeds,
with a BCa interval excluding zero.

**H2 (Early exploit signal adds little).** At reference noise, dropping the day-0 EPSS
observation from the predictor changes MWP@50 by at most 2 points: |MWP@50(PTI-full) -
MWP@50(PTI-noObs)| <= 0.02. That is, stable severity plus the partial KEV flag carry almost all
of the recoverable signal at disclosure time.

**H3 (Mechanism: context drives the gain).** Removing the asset-context factor from the
predictor (setting the context emphasis to zero) erases most of the advantage over the
context-blind baseline: the residual MWP@50 advantage of the context-free predictor is at most
2 points. The gain comes from asset weighting, not from how the predictor treats the exploit
signal.

**H4 (Capacity regime).** The context advantage over the context-blind baseline decays with
remediation capacity: the MWP advantage at K = 50 exceeds that at K = 250.

**Rationale.** The emergent-risk target rewards catching CVEs that will become high exploit-risk
on high-context hosts. At disclosure time the day-0 EPSS observation is a poor estimate of
matured risk, whereas asset criticality is known exactly and aligns directly with the mission
component of the target. We therefore expect context weighting to dominate (H1, H3), the day-0
observation to be nearly redundant given stable signals (H2), and the advantage to follow the
usual capacity decay (H4).

## 3. Maturation and Observation Model

For each (host, CVE) pair, `epss_mat` is the real corpus EPSS (matured value at horizon
H = 30 days) and `kev_mat` the corpus KEV membership.

In-the-wild day-0 observation, noisy:
```
epss_obs = sigmoid( logit(epss_mat) - b + N(0, sigma) ),   clipped to [1e-4, 1]
```
with disclosure-time downward bias `b = 0.75` (logit units, fixed) and noise scale `sigma`,
swept over {0.0, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.0}.

Stable severity correlate, known at disclosure and free of the day-0 noise:
```
cvss_stable = 10 * sigmoid( logit(epss_mat) + N(0, tau) ),  tau = 1.0 fixed
```

Partial KEV-at-disclosure, clean but incomplete (KEV lags disclosure):
```
kev_obs = kev_mat AND (U < 0.3)
```

Emergent-high-risk target (ground truth, fixed across methods). A pair is a Mission-Critical
Emergent True Positive when
```
(epss_mat > 0.10 OR kev_mat) AND ACS(h) > fleet 75th percentile
```
ACS is the weighted Asset Context Score defined in Section 4.

## 4. Method

**Asset Context Score.** Each host carries asset criticality tier, network exposure zone, and
data sensitivity, assigned from fixed publicly-grounded category mixes (FIPS 199 impact levels;
CISA exposure tiers; CJIS and CUI sensitivity). ACS(h) = 0.5 crit + 0.3 zone + 0.2 sens, fixed
from priors.

**Context-weighted predictor (PTI).** A precision-weighted fusion of the de-biased day-0
observation and the stable severity correlate, in logit space, weighted toward the host context:
```
lambda      = (1/sigma^2) / (1/sigma^2 + 1/tau^2)          (lambda = 1 when sigma = 0)
mu_hat(c)   = lambda*(logit(epss_obs)+b) + (1-lambda)*logit(cvss_stable/10) + kev_bonus*kev_obs
S_PTI(h, c) = sigmoid(mu_hat(c)) * (1 + rho * ACS(h))
```
with `b = 0.75`, `tau = 1.0`, `kev_bonus = 2.0`, `rho = 8.0`, all fixed from priors. The fusion
weight uses the known day-0 noise level, which a team estimates from historical EPSS maturation.

**Methods.** EPSS-day0 (context-blind, ranks by `epss_obs`; primary baseline for H1); CAP-day0
(context-weighted day-0 EPSS); EPSS-matured-oracle (`epss_mat`, context-weighted upper bound);
CVSS-only; Random; Context-only (ACS alone); PTI-full; PTI-noObs (drop the day-0 observation,
for H2); PTI-noCrit (rho = 0, no asset weighting, for H3).

---

## 5. Metrics

Primary: mission-weighted precision at K (MWP@K) on the emergent target, K = 50, 100, 250.
Secondary: NDCG@K on the emergent target. Mean with 95% BCa bootstrap interval (10,000
resamples) over 25 seeds; interval non-overlap is the evidentiary standard; no significance
testing. Reference noise sigma = 0.50 for H1, H2, H3.

---

## 6. Failure Criteria

If the context advantage over the context-blind baseline is below 2 points at K = 50 and
sigma = 0.50, declare H1 null and report the noise and capacity characterization instead. If
dropping the day-0 observation changes MWP@50 by more than 5 points, H2 is rejected and the
day-0 signal is reported as materially useful. Exclude any seed with fewer than 50 emergent
positives; if more than 5 of 25 are excluded, halt and report a generation failure. Do not add
post-hoc features or re-tune any constant to reach a threshold.

## 7. Reproducibility

snapshot date.

## 8. Threats and Limitations

The EPSS maturation is simulated from the real matured marginal, not a real EPSS time series, so
absolute numbers are not field measurements and the noise model is a design choice; the
comparative results under the pre-registered sweep are the contribution. The target and the
context weight share the ACS term, mitigated by the context-blind baseline (H1), the criticality
ablation (H3), and fixing the target across methods. The CVSS severity correlate is a modeled
fixed-quality signal that real CVSS only partially meets. One fleet size (about 830 hosts) is
evaluated; capacity and disclosure-time noise are the swept axes.
