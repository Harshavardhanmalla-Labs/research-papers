\subsection{Primary result (H1): partially supported}

Table~\ref{tab:mwp} reports MWP@K for the principal methods. CAP-G beats the base scorer
at \emph{every} capacity, with BCa CIs excluding zero: $+9.5$~pp at $K{=}50$
(CI $[+7.8,+11.3]$), $+3.0$~pp at $K{=}100$ (CI $[+2.3,+3.6]$), and $+0.7$~pp at $K{=}250$
(CI $[+0.5,+0.9]$). The pre-registered H1 bar required the advantage \emph{averaged across
all three $K$} to reach $5$~pp; the realized average is $4.4$~pp, just below threshold. Per
the locked decision rule we report \textbf{H1 as partial}: the benefit is real and
CI-separated at every capacity and exceeds $5$~pp in the triage regime, but it is not a
capacity-flat $5$~pp constant. We deliberately do not re-define the metric or threshold to
convert this to a pass. EPSS-only is a strong MWP competitor at high $K$ ($0.125$ at
$K{=}250$): at large capacity, sweeping all high-EPSS pairs incidentally covers most
high-context ones, itself an instance of capacity decay.

\subsection{Regime dependence (H2): supported}

The advantage decreases monotonically in $K$: $9.5\!\rightarrow\!3.0\!\rightarrow\!0.7$~pp
(Fig.~\ref{fig:decay}). Context-awareness matters most precisely where remediation
capacity is scarce, consistent with the intuition that at high capacity the budget absorbs
most high-priority pairs regardless of ordering.

\subsection{Mechanism (H3): supported}

On a homogeneous control fleet (every host assigned identical context), the CAP-G
advantage is \emph{exactly} $0.0$~pp at every $K$ (CI $[0,0]$). Because a constant context
multiplier preserves rank order (Eq.~\ref{eq:capg}), CAP-G provably reduces to the base scorer
without heterogeneity. The entire heterogeneous-fleet gain is therefore attributable to
fleet context dispersion, not to the scoring transform; the mechanism is confirmed, not
merely consistent.

\subsection{Honest tradeoff (H4): supported}

CAP-G's context-blind P@50 differs from the base scorer's by $+0.1$~pp (CI $[-1.5,+1.6]$),
statistically indistinguishable. CAP-G buys mission precision essentially for free on the
blind metric: it reorders \emph{within} the high-exploit set toward high-context hosts
rather than trading away generic precision. The pre-registered circularity-investigation
rule (triggered only if CAP-G dominated both opposing targets) was checked and not
triggered.

\subsection{Ablation (RQ5)}

Removing each context dimension from the scorer, with the MCTP target held fixed, changes
MWP@50 as shown in Table~\ref{tab:ablation}. Only asset criticality carries
mission-precision signal (CI excludes zero, $-4.0$~pp when removed); network zone and data
sensitivity, at their pre-registered weights, are mild distractors against a
criticality-dominated target. A practitioner takeaway is that a criticality-only context
layer captures essentially all of CAP-G's benefit on this target. NDCG@50 corroborates the
overall picture: CAP-G $0.61$ vs the base scorer $0.34$ (CIs non-overlapping).
