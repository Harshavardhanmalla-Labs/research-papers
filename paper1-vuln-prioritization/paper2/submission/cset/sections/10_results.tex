 \item \textbf{Within-cell variance criterion.} The criterion fires on every primary robustness axis. Each axis is therefore demoted to descriptive-only: although cell-mean differences are large, per-cell seed noise is comparable to the between-cells signal at the 30-seed budget. No sensitivity claim survives this criterion; all results above are descriptive.
\end{itemize}
